How this calculator works

Quick start

  1. Open the Calculator tab and fill in sections 1 to 3 for your vehicle.
  2. Check the results: the box on the right, or the bar at the bottom on phones. It shows your breakeven distance, monthly take-home and payback.
  3. Add more vehicles from the vehicle switcher at the top, then open Compare to see them side by side.

The tabs

  • Calculator: where you enter and edit everything about one vehicle. The vehicle switcher at the top picks which one you're working on.
  • Compare: shows all your vehicles side by side at one shared daily distance. It is view-only.
  • Summary: a shareable card for the selected vehicle. Share it as an image or a link.
  • Trip Calc: a quick calculator for one trip or one day. It is separate from your vehicles.

Where to change things

  • To change a vehicle's price, loan, fuel, mileage or any other number, go to the Calculator tab, pick that vehicle at the top, and edit it there.
  • Compare and Summary update automatically with your changes. You can't edit vehicle details on those tabs.
  • Trip Calc has its own numbers. Changing a vehicle does not change it, and changing it does not change any vehicle.

The Calculator sections

  1. Vehicle & Loan: price, downpayment, loan interest and length, resale value, and the interest your downpayment would have earned in a fixed deposit.
  2. Earnings & Fuel: what you earn per km, working days, fuel type, fuel price and mileage.
  3. Running Costs: maintenance and fixed monthly costs.
  4. Monthly Profit by Daily Distance: how monthly profit changes as you drive more or fewer km.
  5. Daily Run Scenarios (Years 1–5): results for different daily distances over the first five years.
  6. 6-Year Cash Flow at Your Planned Run: what you gain or lose over six years.

Words used here

  • Planned daily run: the distance you expect to drive each day. The slider is shared by all vehicles, unless a vehicle has its own distance turned on.
  • Breakeven: the daily distance at which you cover the loan, overheads and the lost fixed-deposit interest.
  • Take-home (cash): what you keep each month after the loan, fuel, maintenance and overheads.
  • After lost FD interest: take-home minus the interest your downpayment could have earned in a fixed deposit.
  • Payback: the year in which your downpayment is earned back.
  • 6-year net: the total result over six years after the downpayment, including the resale value.

Saving and sharing

  • Your numbers are saved in this browser only. Use Settings → Export backup to keep a copy or move to another device, and Import backup to restore it.
  • A shared link adds the vehicle as a new one for whoever opens it. It never replaces their own vehicles.
  • Trip Calc numbers are temporary and are not included in backups.
